# Vorlagen für Szenenbilder

Erzeuge pro Szene ein eigenständiges Bild. Ersetze die Platzhalter durch Inhalte des deutschen Storyboards. Die sichtbaren Bildwörter müssen exakt und knapp auf Deutsch bleiben.

## Einzelne Erklärvideo-Szene

```text
Create one standalone 16:9 landscape illustration for a German-language explainer video.

Visual style: pure white background, minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly pen strokes, generous blank space, sparse red/orange/blue accents. Clean, absurd product-sketch feeling. No gradient, shadow, paper texture, complex background, commercial vector look, slide or infographic layout, cute mascot poster, children's illustration, or realistic UI.

Recurring character: Xiaohei (小黑), a small solid-black absurd creature with white dot eyes and tiny thin legs, blank serious expression. Xiaohei must perform the core conceptual action, not decorate the scene. Serious and slightly bizarre, never cute.

Scene purpose: {Funktion dieser Szene im Erklärbogen}
Core idea: {eine klare deutsche Kernaussage}
Composition: {Ort, Handlung von Xiaohei, Hauptobjekt und Informationsfluss}
Suggested objects: {ein bis drei passende Elemente}
Exact German labels to render, if any: {höchstens fünf kurze Wörter oder „keine“}

Color: black for main lines and Xiaohei; orange for the main path or movement; red only for a key warning/problem/result; blue sparingly for a secondary note or system state.

Constraints: one scene, one core idea, 16:9. Keep the main subject around 40-60% of the canvas and preserve at least 35% blank white space. Do not add a title, extra labels, English or Chinese text, or any words not specified above. Do not imitate an existing example composition. Invent a fresh visual metaphor specific to this narration. Clear but not instructional, strange but clean, not childish.
```
